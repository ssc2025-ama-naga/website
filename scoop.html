Welcome to SSC 2025!

November 6, 2025

The new Supreme Student Council is officially launched and ready to serve you! We're excited to bring fresh ideas, dynamic events, and meaningful programs to our campus. Whether you want to get involved, attend our events, or simply stay informed, we're here for you. Check out our upcoming events and let's make this year unforgettable!

Sports Fest 2025 - Mark Your Calendars!

November 1, 2025

Get ready for the most exciting sports competition of the year! The annual Sports Fest 2025 will be held from December 1-5 on our campus grounds. We'll have volleyball, basketball, badminton, and many more sporting events. All students are invited to participate and show their support for their batches. More details coming soon!

ANNOUNCEMENT

Leadership Training Workshop

November 1, 2025

All student leaders are invited to our Leadership Training Workshop on November 20! This comprehensive training will cover effective communication, team management, and project planning.
